# 0.1.2 — 2026-09-26

## Added

- **Link previews**: Open Graph and Twitter card tags, a canonical link, and a 1200×630 `og.jpg`.
- **Reload button**: offered on the panel shown after a GPU loss or a failed device.

## Changed

- **Resonator switch**: once the grains land, the last note's figure forms again, silently, so the caption and the vessel agree.
- **Screen readers**: the sound toggle keeps one name and reports its state with `aria-pressed`; figure buttons carry their pitch ("Figure 8, D4"); the caption is announced only in Play; closing the about panel returns focus to the control that opened it.
- **Title page**: a soft halo keeps the copy legible over bright figures on narrow screens.
- **Fullscreen**: the button hides where the browser has no element Fullscreen API (iPhone) and shows whether it will enter or leave.
- **Browser list**: includes Firefox on Apple-silicon Macs.

## Fixed

- **Hidden tab**: notes no longer drone while the page is hidden; the microphone closes and audio suspends. Listen no longer sounds every missed bar at once on return.
- **Sing prompt**: choosing another way while the microphone prompt is up no longer leaves the microphone open.
- **iPhone audio**: sound plays through the ring/silent switch, and audio interrupted by a call or a screen lock resumes on the next tap.
- **Voices**: at most 12 sound at once, and none are synthesized while muted.
- **Touch**: the finger left after a pinch no longer starts a run of notes.
- **Wheel zoom**: honors line and page delta modes (Firefox), and a trackpad pinch zooms at a steady rate.
- **Title page**: letter keys no longer play notes behind it.
- **Display density**: the canvas and figure strip follow a move to a display with a different pixel ratio.
- **Vignette**: smoothstep edges are ordered, which Safari's Metal backend requires.
- **GPU loss**: the frame loop stops and the audio falls silent.
