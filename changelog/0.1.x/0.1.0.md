# 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26

## Added

- **Resonators**: square plate, clamped drum (Bessel modes), sphere (spherical harmonics) and lattice (triply periodic surfaces), with grains flying between them on switch.
- **Grain simulation**: WebGPU compute over ~4 M grains — hops off vibrating regions, Newton-step settling onto nodal lines, onset jolt, pointer stirring, note-color painting.
- **Rendering**: additive points into HDR with light trails, six-level bloom, film tone curve, vignette, fringing, grain; rim outline for flat vessels and a mirror floor under floating ones.
- **Sound**: glass-harmonica voices with a bowed sustain, generated hall reverb, ping-pong delay and D drone via the Web Audio API.
- **Ways to play**: Play (pointer and three keyboard octaves of D Lydian), Listen (generative composer), Sing (microphone pitch detection).
- **Interface**: title page, running figure captions, strip of per-note figure engravings, resonator switch, capture to PNG, full screen, about panel.
- **Tooling**: `bun run check` gate, Vitest suites for the pure logic, `bun run snapshot` headless-Chrome stills.
